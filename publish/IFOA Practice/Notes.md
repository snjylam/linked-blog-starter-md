




**Risk management & controls**
- Three lines of defence (business operations, oversight functions, independent assurance providers).
- Risk categories
	- Credit risk (counterparty/default/derivative)
	- Market risk (asset-liability mismatch)
	- Liquidity risk (cashflow mismatch)
	- Operational risk (conduct/model/parameter)
	- Insurance risk (underwriting/reinsurance)
	- Group risk
	- Climate risk (physical/transition/liability)

**Product design factors**
- Profitability
- Marketability
- Suitability to meet needs of target market
- Competitiveness
- Distribution method and remuneration
- Financing / capital requirements
- Onerousness of any options and guarantees
- Sensitivity of profit
- Extent of cross-subsidies
- Administration systems
- Service standards
- Company reputation
- Treating customers fairly
- Level of risk
- Underwriting philosophy
- Reinsurance terms and capacity
- Taxation
- Other legal and regulator requirements 
- Environmental, social, and governance (ESG) considerations.
