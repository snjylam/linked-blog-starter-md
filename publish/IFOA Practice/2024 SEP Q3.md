<u><b>Q3(ii): Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of level and decreasing term assurance business for policyholder.</b></u>
**Level term assurance and decreasing term assurance**
**Advantages**
- Term assurances guarantee to pay a specified amount on death.
- They are a cheap way to obtain life cover.
- It is a simple product so is likely to be fair.
- It is easy for the customer to understand what they are buying.
- The simple design makes it reasonably easy to sell online.
- The policyholder has the option to add critical illness cover if they want it.

**Disadvantages**
- There is no surrender value or maturity value.
- There is not much flexibility (e.g. to change the sum assured);
- so the policyholder may end up over-insuring themselves.
- The real value of the sum assured will be eroded by inflation.
- The sum assured may not keep pace with the amount the policyholder has borrowed, e.g. if the amount borrowed under a mortgage is increased.
- There is no allowance for healthy lifestyle other than the original underwriting

**Decreasing term assurance**
**Advantages**
- The decreasing term assurance will be cheaper than the level term assurance (for a given level of initial sum assured).
- The decreasing term assurance is more suitable than the level term assurance for certain types of customer need.
- If the sum assured decreases in the same way as the outstanding balance of a mortgage, it avoids paying for more life cover than is required.

**Disadvantages**
- Premiums can seem high compared to the sum assured late on in the term.
- The sum assured is low near the end of the policy.
