- in the expectation of significantly reduced future administration costs;
- as simpler to administer.
- There may be options to enable changes via court schemes;
- but these are likely to be costly;
- and take time.
- Could cause reputational damage if not favourable to customers.
- How will Company A assess that policyholders are being treated fairly?

- Care needed to consider different subgroups of policyholder to see if any will be materially worse off by change?

- and project costs.

- Changes could impact not only amount of profit emerging for existing business but also the timing of the emergence of that profit.
